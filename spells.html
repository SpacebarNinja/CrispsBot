Spells
SpellWhat it does
Shield Reaction — when you're about to be hit, instantly raise a magical barrier that gives +5 AC until your next turn
Sleep Knocks creatures unconscious in a targeted area, affecting weaker creatures first (works by total HP, not their choice)
Mage Armor Touch yourself or an ally wearing no armor — their AC becomes 13 + DEX modifier until you cast it again

Tasha's Hideous Laughter Target creature finds everything hilarious — they fall Prone and are Incapacitated (can't act), repeating a save each turn
Cloud of Daggers Fill a 5-foot cube with spinning daggers at a point within 60 ft — any creature that enters or starts its turn there takes 4d4 slashing damage
Misty Step Bonus action — surrounded by silvery mist, teleport up to 30 feet to any unoccupied space you can see

Spells

Entangle Cover a 20 ft area in grasping vines; creatures inside can be Restrained (can't move) unless they break free each turn
Faerie Fire Outline creatures in glowing light — they can't benefit from being invisible, and all attacks against them have Advantage

Steria

Kalashtar Paladin  ·  Level 2  ·  2 × 1st-Level Spell Slots

Paladin Features
FeatureWhat it does
Divine Sense Action — sense any Celestial, Fiend, or Undead within 60 ft until the end of your next turn. You know their location and type (not identity). Usable (1 + CHA mod) times per long rest
Lay on Hands Touch a creature to restore HP from your healing pool of 10 HP (refills on long rest). Alternatively, spend 5 HP from the pool to cure one disease or neutralize one poison affecting them
Divine Smite After hitting with a melee weapon, expend a spell slot to add +2d8 radiant damage (1st-level slot). Devastating burst damage — triggers after the hit is confirmed
Fighting Style Passive bonus from your training (e.g. Defense: +1 AC while wearing armor, or Dueling: +2 damage when wielding a one-handed weapon and nothing in your other hand)
Prepared Spells  (1st-Level Slots: 2)
SpellWhat it does
Command Action — one-word order (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel, Halt) to a creature within 60 ft that can hear you. They must obey on their next turn. WIS save negates
Ceremony 1-hour ritual (no spell slot cost) — perform a sacred rite such as Coming of Age (creature adds 1d4 to ability checks for 24 hrs), Dedication (creature gains resistance to one damage type for 24 hrs), or Bless Water (create holy water)
Divine Favor Bonus action — Concentration, 1 min. Your weapon attacks deal an extra +1d4 radiant damage on every hit. Stacks well with Divine Smite
Wrathful Smite Bonus action before attacking — Concentration, 1 min. Next melee hit adds +1d6 psychic damage, and the target must make a WIS save or become Frightened of you until the spell ends
Searing Smite Bonus action before attacking — Concentration, 1 min. Next melee hit adds +1d6 fire damage and sets the target ablaze — they take 1d6 fire damage at the start of each of their turns (CON save each turn to extinguish)
Kalashtar Abilities
AbilityWhat it does
Mind Link Bonus action — establish a telepathic link with one creature you can see within 10 ft. Lasts 1 hour. You can send and receive thoughts without speaking. The target can respond if they share a language with you. Only one link active at a time
Dual Mind Passive — Advantage on all Wisdom saving throws. Your dual soul nature makes mental effects far harder to force upon you
Mental Discipline Passive — Resistance to psychic damage. You take half damage from all psychic sources
Severed from Dreams You do not dream when you sleep. You are immune to magic that requires the target to be dreaming (e.g. Dream spell). You also have Advantage on saving throws against effects that impose the dreaming condition

Aeran

Ranger  ·  Level 2  ·  2 × 1st-Level Spell Slots

Spells
SpellWhat it does
Hunter's Mark Bonus action — mark a creature you can see. You deal +1d6 damage to it on every weapon attack, and you have Advantage on Perception and Survival checks to find it. Concentration, up to 1 hour. If the target dies, you can move the mark to a new creature as a bonus action
Cure Wounds Touch a creature to restore 1d8 + WIS modifier HP. Can't be used on undead or constructs
